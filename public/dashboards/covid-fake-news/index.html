What that means for the accuracy figures

Because almost every source is entirely fake or entirely true, a model can score well by recognising each publisher's house style rather than misinformation itself. The accuracy figures here are best read as how well style separates these publishers. A fair test would hold out whole sources, so the model meets publishers it has never seen.
