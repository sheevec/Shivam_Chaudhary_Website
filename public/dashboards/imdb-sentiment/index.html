Words that push a review positive

Logistic regression weights for the strongest positive words and phrases, after lemmatization ("loved" and "loves" count as "love").

Words that push a review negative

The negative side is led by "—", with a weight of —, larger than any positive word. Words like "even" and "instead" signal disappointment rather than anything bad on their own.
